Routinely – Recurring Issues for Jira

Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Routinely (“the app”) is an Atlassian Forge app published by Marouani Apps (Mohamed Habib Marouani) (“we”, “us”).

In short
  • The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge and is eligible for the Runs on Atlassian program. We operate no servers and your data is never sent to us.
  • It makes no requests outside Atlassian — only to your own Jira site.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or advertising.

1. Data the app processes

DataWhySource
Schedule settings: name, project, issue type, summary and description templates, labels, priority, due date rule, recurrence and time zoneCreate the issues you configuredEntered by users
Account IDs of the schedule owner and the default assigneeSet reporter/assignee and enforce who can edit a scheduleJira
Run log: time, schedule name, created issue key or error message (last 500 runs)Show what was created and troubleshoot failuresCreated by the app

When it creates an issue, the app reads the fields of the template issue you choose (if any) to copy them. It does not store issue contents beyond your own schedule templates.

2. Where data is stored

All data is stored in Atlassian Forge storage for your installation, hosted by Atlassian. We have no access to it.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Schedules are kept until you delete them.
  • The run log keeps the most recent 500 runs.
  • Every 3 days the app uses Atlassian's personal data reporting API and removes references to closed Atlassian accounts.
  • When you uninstall the app, Atlassian deletes its Forge storage according to its Forge data-retention rules.

4. Sub-processors

Atlassian Pty Ltd (Forge platform). No others.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

Your organization is the data controller. Because we cannot access your data, requests are handled by your Jira administrators (edit or delete schedules, or uninstall the app). For questions, contact us below.
